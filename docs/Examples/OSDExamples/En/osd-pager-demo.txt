Dante Alighieri — The Divine Comedy, Inferno, Canto I

Midway upon the journey of our life I found myself within a forest dark, for the straightforward pathway had been lost. Ah me! how hard a thing it is to say what was this forest savage and harsh and stubborn, which in the very thought renews the fear. So bitter is it, death is little more; but of the good to treat, which there I found, I'll speak of the other things I saw there. I cannot well repeat how I entered it, so full of slumber was I at the point where I abandoned the true way.
