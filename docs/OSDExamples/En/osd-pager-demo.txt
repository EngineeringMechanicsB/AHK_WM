The North Wind and the Sun — Aesop's Fable

The North Wind and the Sun had a quarrel about which of them was the stronger. While they were disputing, a Traveler passed along the road wrapped in a cloak.

"Let us agree," said the Sun, "that he is the stronger who can strip that Traveler of his cloak."

"Very well," growled the North Wind, and at once sent a cold, howling blast against the Traveler. But the harder he blew, the more closely did the Traveler wrap his cloak around him.

Then the Sun began to shine. At first his beams were gentle, and the Traveler loosened his cloak. Then the Sun shone brighter, and the Traveler removed his cloak. So the Sun was declared the winner.

Moral: Persuasion is better than force.
